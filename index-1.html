How it works

Four simple steps. No confusing menus for the person taking the medicine.

1

The caregiver adds the medicines

Type the medicine name, choose 💊 tablets or 🧴 liquid in ml, set how much per dose, then pick the times and days. A 4-digit PIN keeps the patient from changing anything by accident.

2

The patient gets a clear reminder

At dose time you get a reminder notification. The screen shows only what matters: the medicine, how much to take, the time, and one giant green TAKEN button.

3

Two taps to be sure

Tapping TAKEN opens a friendly question, “Did you take it?”, followed by a second “Please confirm once more”. No accidental taps, no wrong history.

4

Missed a dose? Everyone gets a warning

An hour after a dose is due, the patient sees a bold red warning with Call and Text buttons, and the caregiver’s alert shows the patient’s number ready to dial. A call button floats on every screen, always.

Questions

How do I install it?

Tap “Install the app”. On Android Chrome press Install. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. It then opens like any other app. A Google Play version is planned.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once installed, the app opens and works without internet. Reminders appear while the app is running; a full Android app with background reminders is coming.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Can the caregiver see doses on their own phone?

Not yet. Today each phone shows what is recorded on that phone. Live family sync is coming soon.

Does PillPal replace my doctor?

No. PillPal is a reminder tool and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor’s instructions.
